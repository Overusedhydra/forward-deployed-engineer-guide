# The Forward Deployed Engineer — Visual Learning Guide

A beautifully designed, ADHD-friendly visual book that makes Fan Bing's comprehensive guide to the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) profession accessible to English-speaking audiences.

## What is FDE?

**95% of enterprise AI projects fail.** Models are no longer scarce — the people who can embed models into real customer businesses are. Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) are those people: engineers stationed at customer sites who bridge the gap between what AI products can do and what customers actually need.
