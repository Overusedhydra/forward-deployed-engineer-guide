# The Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) — Visual Book & English Translation

**The complete guide to the Forward Deployed Engineer role — now in English, beautifully designed for the web.**

## What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is an engineer stationed at the customer site who bridges the gap between what AI products can do and what customers actually need. Born at Palantir, now hired by OpenAI, Anthropic, and hundreds of AI startups — FDE is the hottest role in tech.

**95% of enterprise AI projects fail.** Models are no longer scarce — the people who can embed models into real customer businesses are.

## Why This Repository

The original book was written in Chinese by [Fan Bing (XDash)](https://github.com/xdash/FDE-the-Guidance-Book-of-Forward-Deployed-Engineer). This repository makes it accessible to English-speaking audiences through:

- **Complete English translation** — all 8 chapters + appendices, every word
- **Visual learning design** — stat cards, callout boxes, comparison tables, quote blocks
- **Dark mode** — toggle with the ◐ button, respects your system preference
- **Fully responsive** — read on any device, from phone to widescreen
- **Zero dependencies** — single HTML file, no server needed
