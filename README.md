# The Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) — Visual Book

**The complete guide to the Forward Deployed Engineer role — in English, designed for the web.**

## What is FDE?

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) is an engineer stationed at the customer site who bridges the gap between what AI products can do and what customers actually need. Born at Palantir, now hired by OpenAI, Anthropic, and hundreds of AI startups.

**95% of enterprise AI projects fail.** Models are no longer scarce — the people who can embed models into real customer businesses are.
